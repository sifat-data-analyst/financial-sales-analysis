# Financial Sales Analysis
### Turning Sales Data into Actionable Business Insights

## Project Overview

Businesses need more than sales figures to make informed decisions. They need to understand where revenue comes from, which products generate profit, how regions perform, and where improvement opportunities exist.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end sales analysis workflow using Microsoft Excel to transform transaction-level data into business insights and an interactive performance dashboard.

## Business Problem

The objective is to help business stakeholders understand:

- Which product categories generate the most revenue and profit?
- Which regions perform best and which need improvement?
- How do customer segments contribute to sales?
- How has sales performance changed over time?
- Where should management investigate potential profitability issues?

## Project Objectives

1. Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
2. Identify high-performing and low-performing categories.
3. Compare regional sales and profit performance.
4. Understand customer segment contributions.
5. Analyze historical sales trends.
6. Present findings through an interactive dashboard.
7. Provide actionable recommendations for business decisions.

## Key Business Insights

### Sales Performance

- Technology generated the highest category sales: $836,154.03.
- West was the leading region with $725,457.82 in sales.
- Consumer was the largest customer segment, generating $1,161,401.35.
- 2017 recorded the highest annual sales: $733,215.26.

### Profitability

- Technology generated the highest category profit: $145,454.95.
- West recorded the highest regional profit: $108,418.45.
- Furniture generated comparatively low profit of $18,451.27 despite approximately $742,000 in sales.

### Business Opportunity

Furniture profitability deserves further investigation. Management could review product-level costs, discount policies, and pricing to identify improvement opportunities.
