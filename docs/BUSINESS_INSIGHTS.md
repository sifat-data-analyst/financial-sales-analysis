# Financial Sales Analysis — Business Insights

## 1. Executive Summary

The analysis covers 9,994 sales records from 2014 to 2017. Total sales reached $2,297,200.86, generating $286,397.02 in profit.

The overall profit margin was 12.47%.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $2,297,200.86 |
| Total Profit | $286,397.02 |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |
| Total Customers | 793 |
| Profit Margin | 12.47% |

## 3. Sales Performance Insights

### Category Performance

- Technology generated the highest sales: $836,154.03.
- Furniture generated $742,000.00 in sales.
- Office Supplies generated $719,047.03 in sales.

### Regional Performance

- West recorded the highest sales: $725,457.82.
- East followed with $678,781.24.
- West also generated the highest profit: $108,418.45.
- Central recorded the lowest regional profit: $39,706.36.

### Yearly Performance

- 2017 was the strongest sales year, generating $733,215.26.
- 2016 generated $609,205.60.
- 2014 and 2015 generated $484,247.50 and $470,532.51 respectively.

### Customer Segment

- Consumer was the largest sales segment, generating $1,161,401.35.
- Corporate generated $706,146.37.
- Home Office generated $429,653.15.

## 4. Profitability Insights

- Technology generated the highest category profit: $145,454.95.
- Office Supplies generated $122,490.80 in profit.
- Furniture generated $18,451.27 in profit.
- The Furniture category deserves further investigation to understand its comparatively low profitability.

## 5. Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the factors contributing to strong Technology sales and profitability.
2. Review Furniture pricing, discounting, and product-level costs.
3. Study successful sales practices in the West region.
4. Investigate the profitability gap between West and Central regions.
5. Analyze Consumer purchasing patterns to identify opportunities for customer retention and growth.
6. Review yearly sales trends to support future sales planning.

## 6. Analytical Scope

These findings describe historical sales performance. They do not establish causation or guarantee future results.

Recommendations should be evaluated with additional product, pricing, discount, shipping, and customer-level analysis.
