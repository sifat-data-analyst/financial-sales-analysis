# Financial Sales Analysis — Limitations

## 1. Dataset Source and Licensing

- The original dataset source has not yet been identified.
- Redistribution and public sharing permissions have not been confirmed.
- The original dataset-containing workbook must not be published publicly until licensing permission is verified.

## 2. Historical Data Scope

- The analysis covers historical sales transactions from 2014 to 2017.
- Findings describe the available historical data and should not be interpreted as forecasts.
- Business conditions may have changed since the period covered.

## 3. Analytical Scope

- The project focuses on descriptive and diagnostic sales analysis.
- The analysis identifies patterns and relationships but does not establish causation.
- Additional investigation may be needed to explain differences in profitability.

## 4. Data Quality and Validation

- Product Name formatting differences were identified between the raw and clean worksheets.
- Repeated Order IDs and Customer IDs are valid transaction-level occurrences and should not automatically be considered duplicate records.
- Formula checks and KPI comparisons were completed, but independent review of every business interpretation remains outside the current validation scope.

## 5. Dashboard Review

- The user reported that all four dashboard slicers were functioning.
- Final visual inspection and screenshot review remain pending.

## 6. Business Recommendations

Recommendations are based on the available historical dataset. They should be evaluated alongside current business information, operational costs, pricing policies, and other relevant factors before implementation.
